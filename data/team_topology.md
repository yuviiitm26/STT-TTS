# Engineering Team Topology and Ownership

Engineering at AcmeCorp is divided into three main squads.

**Squad Alpha (Infrastructure & Gateway):**
Led by Sarah Jenkins. This team is responsible for the Kubernetes clusters, CI/CD pipelines, and the Kong API Gateway. 

**Squad Beta (Core Commerce):**
Led by David Chen. Squad Beta maintains the Auth Service and the Order Service. The lead backend engineer for the Order Service is Marcus Thorne. 

**Squad Gamma (Fulfillment & Catalog):**
Led by Elena Rostova. This team manages the Catalog Service, the Inventory Service, and all associated databases including MongoDB.
